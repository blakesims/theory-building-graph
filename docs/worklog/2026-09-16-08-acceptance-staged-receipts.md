# Scoped staged evidence

Historical note, 2026-09-16. Original path: `acceptance/STAGED-RECEIPTS.md`.

Scoped receipts must bind the complete parent grade, disclose its failures and retain every criterion assigned to their case. Every recorded stage retains prompt, stream, receipt and before/after graph hashes, with continuity checked. A fork preserves copied stages and their original contract versions, not a retrospective upgraded trial. Partial components cannot establish a whole case alone. Composite rubrics bind required evidence slots, component criteria and parent disclosures recursively. Tampered hashes, missing failed stages or narrowed parent rubrics fail closed. These are integrity checks, not independent semantic regrading.
