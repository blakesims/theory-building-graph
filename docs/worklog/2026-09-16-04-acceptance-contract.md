# Acceptance contract for an agent-assisted theory graph

Historical note, 2026-09-16. Original path: `acceptance/CONTRACT.md`.

Entities and operations name the modeled world. Claims, questions and extractions describe it. Roles are not session instances. Permission, deciding, performing and recording are distinct. Formal patterns are authored interpretations with separate standing. Unknown scope, identity or role overlap must remain unknown. Only explicit dependencies govern readiness. Withdrawal cannot erase a prerequisite. Revisions preserve former claims and rationale. Authority comparisons distinguish may, must, only and never. Different exclusive roles alone do not establish a contradiction. Cardinality counts distinct identities, needs completeness for absence, and needs subject existence for global inconsistency. Finite traces preserve event-time roles and provenance. Missing evidence is not false or zero. Synthetic evidence never establishes runtime history. Agent trials require exact source artifacts, independent semantic grades, honest costs and disclosed failures. Stopping authority and general cancellation remain user decisions, not fixture answers.
